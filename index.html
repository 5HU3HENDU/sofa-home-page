How it fits: the whole desk is drawn once at a fixed size, then scaled to your exact screen — no scrolling, nothing cut off. How it stays crisp: every object is vector, so it never blurs, and tapping a cluster zooms it to full sharpness while the rest dims. What got cut: 15 repeated icons — the 14 identical Community bubbles are now one emblem + compact pills, and no topic emblem repeats one of its own subtopics. A few generic icons (heart, ring) still do double duty as vocabulary, not as repeats.

F · Isometric Room

The same desk, seen from above at an angle — a little 3D world. Iso floor and furniture, the flat objects standing on them, highlights floating above the scene. Tap any object to see its topic.

In this room: train + flight tickets → Travel · wallet + bill → Wealth · dog + cat → Pets · TV → Content · coffee → People · key → Home · bottle → Health · badge → Career · phone → Tech · document → Legal · toolbox → Life Skills · gift → Life Events · jacket → Fashion · chat → Community. The bill glows with its sticky note; the chat floats with its NEW badge.

Best at: depth and wow — it feels like a little world you could walk into, the most game-like of all six boards. Risk: the hardest to keep readable on a phone (objects shrink as the scene scales), and the most illustration work to do properly. Highlights need the floating treatment — glow alone drowns in a busy scene.

How "important" could look

Three treatments, same rule for all of them: at most 1–2 highlighted at once, and always with a reason. Tap any object anywhere on this page to see its topic.

Glow + sticky note
Soft pulse, plus a note saying why: "Tax season — due soon". Warm, hard to miss.

Dot + tag
A pulsing dot and a NEW tag. Quiet, works in a grid without shouting.

Front of stage
The object gets big, floats gently, and carries one line explaining why this week.

My read, for when you're ready to pick: B (the shelf) is the safest real homepage — it works on a phone and never confuses anyone. C (hero + shelf) is the most School of Adults: it gives the site a voice that says "this week, this matters". A is the most lovable and the hardest to keep tidy. D is a lovely second screen, not a homepage.
